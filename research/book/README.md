# Research book

The research book is the academic narrative layer of the repository. It records
the question, chronology, evidence, decisions, interpretation, and limitations
in readable form while linking every quantitative claim to reproducible output.

- `SIM-02.md` — active thermopolarization chapter.

Executable calculations and interactive figures live in the Marimo notebooks.
Frozen methods and results live in `results/reports/`. The book explains how the
evidence changed the research direction; it does not replace the raw evidence.
